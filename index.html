CHAMPIONSHIP

THE FIGHT FOR EVERY POINT

The FIA Formula One World Championship is the highest class of single-seater auto racing. It is a battle of speed,startegy and consistency. Drivers and teams compete throughout the season to claim the championship.

CHAMPIONSHIP FACTS

  • 22 Drivers compete for the Drivers' Championship
  • 11 Teams compete for the Constructors' Championship
  • The top 10 drivers are awarded points at every Grand Prix
  • The season takes place across 23 different countries
